# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): major.minor.patch. `python3 tools/release.py patch|minor|major` sets the next one and adds its heading here.

## 1.4.0

- 🖼️ Souvenir picture: the story's name and date, and up to three moments (😮 surprises, 📝 notes, the deepest answers).
- 💾 Save all our stories in one .loopy file from "Our stories", and open it with 📂 to bring them back, here or on another device (the newer copy of a story wins).
- At the end of a story worth keeping, a reminder to save it to a file, since it only lives in this browser.
- Cards keep only 👎 "Not for us" (the question is replaced and never comes back on this device); 👍 and the ratings export are gone.

## 1.3.1

- 🔠 Text size: a bar slides up from the bottom, with a slider (80 to 200 %) and one-tap sizes (100, 125, 150, 200 %); 100 % is always one tap away.
- The 🔊 and ? buttons on the card have their icon centred.
- The SPIN button keeps its size when the text is bigger.

## 1.3.0

- Five new subjects, 140 questions: 🎬 Pop culture, ⚽ Sport & movement, 💰 Money & spending, 🍋 The five senses, 🌟 What matters (1,560 questions and votes in all).
- 💬 Follow-up on every card: a way to go further on the same question (why, an example, the story behind it).
- 💛 Sensitive questions (grief, heartbreak, shame…) say that passing is always fine.
- 👍 / 👎 on every card, kept on the device: 👎 replaces the question and never brings it back there. The version panel can save the ratings to a file.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 All ages option: light or medium questions only, no love subject, no heavy or grown-up question.
- 24 near-duplicate questions rewritten (the speed, date and couple pools no longer repeat the subjects).

## 1.2.0

- ⚡ Play now: from step 1, the wheel with every subject, nothing to set (suggested on a first visit).
- The options are folded under Options on step 3; the essentials (subjects, depth) stay in view.
- Tablet: 🔃 turns the card around for the person across the table; bigger card and wheel on big screens.
- Computer: full screen, and the keyboard in every game (Space spin or draw, Enter answered, P pass, R read aloud, V turn the card, J our story, F full screen).
- 🔠 Text size (100 to 150 %), in the game and on the home screen.
- With "reduce motion" turned on in the device, the wheel spins in about a second.

## 1.1.2

- Automatic tests on every change: the question bank, the version and offline files, and a browser that plays every game.
- A preview image and description when the link is shared (WhatsApp, Discord, Messenger…).
- Licence: free to play, all rights reserved.

## 1.1.1

- Smoother wheel: its face is drawn once and turned by the graphics card, instead of being repainted on every frame (it shook on some computers).

## 1.1.0

- New "💼 Between colleagues" games: ☕ Coffee break (2 to 8 colleagues, the wheel with work-life questions) and 🎤 Icebreaker (a whole team on a shared screen, in a video call or a meeting room: one question, everyone answers in turn, driven from the keyboard, optional countdown per person, full screen).
- 120 work-life questions (career path, ways of working, office stories, hidden talents), light or medium only; between colleagues, the other subjects stay light and the too personal ones are left out.

## 1.0.0

First version with a standard number (the app showed v1 to v27 before).

- 9 games: the wheel, the tree, truth or bluff, duel, the bet, first date, couple, car and speed.
- 993 questions in 19 subjects, in English and French, plus first date, couple, +18 and speed questions, votes and challenges.
- Options: guess for each other, "who of us?" votes, challenges, random next player, read the questions aloud (and 🔊 on every card).
- Every story kept on the device (Our stories), .loopy files, badges, shareable picture, offline app with an update banner.
