# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/): major.minor.patch. `python3 tools/release.py patch|minor|major` sets the next one and adds its heading here.

## 1.1.0

- New "💼 Between colleagues" games: ☕ Coffee break (2 to 8 colleagues, the wheel with work-life questions) and 🎤 Icebreaker (a whole team on a shared screen, in a video call or a meeting room: one question, everyone answers in turn, driven from the keyboard, optional countdown per person, full screen).
- 120 work-life questions (career path, ways of working, office stories, hidden talents), light or medium only; between colleagues, the other subjects stay light and the too personal ones are left out.

## 1.0.0

First version with a standard number (the app showed v1 to v27 before).

- 9 games: the wheel, the tree, truth or bluff, duel, the bet, first date, couple, car and speed.
- 993 questions in 19 subjects, in English and French, plus first date, couple, +18 and speed questions, votes and challenges.
- Options: guess for each other, "who of us?" votes, challenges, random next player, read the questions aloud (and 🔊 on every card).
- Every story kept on the device (Our stories), .loopy files, badges, shareable picture, offline app with an update banner.
